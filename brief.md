# Nostalgie, Teplice — website brief

Research date: 28 October 2026. Uncommissioned wireframe concept, not an approved restaurant website.

## Objective
Help nearby visitors decide what to eat, find the restaurant, and call to arrange a table. Primary action: read the menu directly on the website. Secondary actions: call and get directions.

## Audience and proposed positioning
Local lunch visitors, Teplice visitors, friends meeting for dinner, and people arranging small social gatherings. Proposed tone: welcoming, straightforward Czech; an approachable neighbourhood restaurant rather than an invented luxury dining brand. Audience and positioning are design hypotheses to discuss with the owner.

## Verified online presence
- [Firmy.cz](https://www.firmy.cz/detail/2358725-restaurace-nostalgie-teplice.html): address U Kamenných lázní 657/5, 415 01 Teplice; phone +420 773 451 825; steaks, pizza, salads and social events. Also displays a dated menu from 26 October 2026.
- [Meníčka.cz](https://www.menicka.cz/3342-nostalgie.html): dedicated lunch-menu listing for Nostalgie at the matching address. Search and direct responses exposed differing older dates; do not treat retrieved dishes as today's menu.
- [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/p/RESTAURACE-NOSTALGIE-100063502664472/): the restaurant page linked by Firmy.cz. Direct Facebook access returned a temporary block, so current posts and page branding were not verified.
- [Restaurant Guru menu archive](https://restaurantguru.com/Restaurace-Nostalgie-Teplice/menu): user-uploaded menu photographs exist; not reliable enough for a current permanent menu transcription.

## Information architecture
1. Header: restaurant name, menu, restaurant, contact, telephone reservation.
2. Hero: short location-specific introduction and primary menu action; large restaurant photograph placeholder.
3. Menu: readable text layout, explicitly dated archive example, on-page dishes and prices; adjacent steak, pizza and salad categories.
4. Restaurant: short introduction and interior photograph placeholder.
5. Gallery: food, table detail, entrance placeholders.
6. Reservation callout: phone call, no unsupported booking backend.
7. Contact: verified address and phone, telephone enquiry for current opening hours, map placeholder linking to directions.
8. Mobile bottom bar: menu and phone access.

## Visual specification
Low-fidelity monochrome wireframe. Editorial headings and restrained spacing establish hierarchy without selecting a final identity. Crossed image boxes explicitly describe the required photograph. Responsive desktop and mobile structure; no stock photos, fake reviews, invented awards or restaurant history.

## Menu and content handling
The example dishes and prices are attributed to the 26 October archive shown by Firmy.cz, clearly marked as historical. In production, agree one source of truth with the restaurant: owner-maintained dated text or an authorised feed. Do not present stale menu data as current. Facebook and Meníčka.cz are research sources only. Do not advertise, embed or link to either in the visitor experience. The restaurant website owns the on-page menu presentation; source citations remain in this brief. This prototype does not scrape or automatically synchronise menus.

## Owner questions before launch
Confirm weekly and holiday opening hours (directories conflict), kitchen and lunch service hours, permanent menu and allergens, image permissions, logo, event capacity and reservation procedure. Confirm whether the restaurant wants additional languages. No delivery, online payment, online booking, parking or accessibility claims are made.

## Acceptance criteria
Menu, telephone and directions are discoverable immediately; historical menu is clearly labelled; narrow screens stack cleanly; no external fonts or imagery are required. Telephone links open the dialler, menu navigation stays on the website. No live business changes or publication are part of this draft.

## Styling direction
Warm cream paper, deep burgundy, muted brass and olive, drawn from wood, brick, warm walls and vintage motorcycle details in public photographs. References: https://www.apetee.com/Nostalgie-Teplice and https://www.menicka.cz/3342-nostalgie.html . Photo placeholders retained. Shared style.css keeps the homepage and menu consistent.
